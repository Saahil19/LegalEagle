CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024,
by and between TechVentures Global LLC, a Delaware corporation ("Company"),
and Alex Morgan, an individual residing in California ("Consultant").

1. SERVICES
Consultant agrees to provide software development and AI research consulting
services as directed by Company from time to time. Company may unilaterally
modify the scope of services without prior notice to Consultant.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION
This Agreement shall commence on January 15, 2024. Company may terminate this
Agreement immediately, at any time, without cause, without written notice, and
without any obligation to pay for work already completed but not yet invoiced.
Consultant may only terminate this Agreement upon providing 180 days prior
written notice to Company.

3. COMPENSATION
Consultant shall be paid $50 per hour for services rendered. Company reserves
the right to reduce this rate at any time with 7 days notice. Company shall
withhold final payment of any amounts owed until all deliverables have been
accepted in writing by Company at its sole and absolute discretion.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY OWNERSHIP
All work product, inventions, discoveries, developments, improvements,
software, code, documentation, designs, and any other materials created by
Consultant during or related to this engagement, whether during business hours
or personal time, whether using Company resources or Consultant's own resources,
shall be the sole and exclusive property of Company. Consultant hereby
irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in all such work product to
Company. This assignment extends to all prior inventions that Consultant has
created in the five years prior to signing this Agreement that are related to
Company's business in any way.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Company, its officers,
directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns from and against any and
all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including
reasonable attorney's fees) arising out of or related to: (a) Consultant's
performance of services; (b) any breach of this Agreement by Consultant;
(c) any third-party claims related to Consultant's work product; or (d) any
act or omission by Consultant. Company shall have no obligation to indemnify
Consultant for any reason whatsoever.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Consultant agrees to keep strictly confidential all information related to
Company's business, technology, customers, financials, and strategy. This
confidentiality obligation shall remain in effect for a period of ten (10) years
after the termination of this Agreement. Consultant may not disclose any
information to any third party under any circumstances, including in legal
proceedings, without express written consent from Company's CEO.

7. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION
For a period of five (5) years following the termination of this Agreement,
Consultant shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the world:
(a) engage in, own, manage, operate, consult for, or be employed by any
business that competes with Company or any of its affiliates in any way;
(b) solicit, hire, or engage any employee, contractor, or customer of Company;
(c) develop any product or service that could potentially compete with any
current or future product of Company.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the Cayman Islands,
without regard to conflict of law principles. All disputes shall be resolved
by binding arbitration in the Cayman Islands. Consultant waives any right to
bring a class action or join any class action against Company. Consultant
further waives the right to a jury trial for any dispute arising under this
Agreement.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
In no event shall Company be liable to Consultant for any damages of any kind,
including direct, indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages,
regardless of the cause of action or theory of liability. Company's maximum
liability to Consultant shall not exceed $100 regardless of the circumstances.

10. MISCELLANEOUS
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties. Company
may modify this Agreement at any time by posting an updated version online,
and Consultant's continued performance of services shall constitute acceptance
of such modifications. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining
provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
